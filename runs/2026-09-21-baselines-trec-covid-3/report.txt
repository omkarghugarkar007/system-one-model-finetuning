  The decision this table informs: FrontierRank does not need the
  best reranker, it needs a CALIBRATED one, because expected-regret
  arithmetic runs on probabilities and not on scores. If Laya loses
  quality but wins calibration, the architecture stands and the
  question becomes what the quality gap costs. If it loses both,
  a pretrained cross-encoder is the better substrate and plan.md's
  own Part XI decision tree already says to use one.
